Accounting Formulas & Data Integrity

1. Net Sales Revenue = Gross Sales − Discounts − Refunds
2. Total COGS = Recorded Product COGS − Reversed Refund COGS + Redeemed QR Gift Costs
3. Gross Profit = Net Sales Revenue − Total COGS
4. Net Profit / Loss = Gross Profit − Operating Expenses
Note: Sales revenue received via UPI or Cash is gross tender, never labeled as profit. Unredeemed QR slips incur ₹0 gift cost until verified as Collected.
